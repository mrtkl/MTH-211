Final exam review: Chapters 1–5

What this covers. Every chapter of MTH 211. The final exam asks you to compute, to model (with the manipulatives from class) and to explain why procedures work, so read each worked solution even when your answer is correct.

Suggested study plan. (1) Take the comprehensive practice final without notes and note which topics you miss. (2) Redo the chapter final quizzes for those topics and reopen the chapter outlines and their manipulatives. (3) Use the Final Practice Lab for new problems until each topic feels routine. (4) Review the Self-Study Quiz in each chapter outline.
